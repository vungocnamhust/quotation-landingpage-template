CHAPTER 02 · DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY

Day-by-Day Journey Program

Your private 11D10N journey — 11 days, carefully crafted.

Hanoi DAY 1

Day 1 — Hanoi

Upon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport, meet your private guide and driver before transferring to Sheraton Hanoi. Enjoy a relaxing afternoon after your international flight. Depending on your arrival time, you may take a leisurely walk around West Lake or enjoy the hotel’s facilities.

Highlights: Private airport transfer, English-speaking guide, Sheraton Hanoi check-in.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Relaxed
Overnight
Hanoi
Meals
None
Sapa DAY 2

Day 2 — Sapa

Breakfast at hotel. Depart for Sapa via the Hanoi – Lao Cai Expressway (approximately 5.5–6 hours). Upon arrival, check in at Hotel de la Coupole MGallery Sapa, one of Vietnam’s most iconic luxury mountain resorts. Free afternoon to relax or explore Sapa town.

Highlights: Private AC transfer via Expressway, Hotel de la Coupole Sapa check-in, guide.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Moderate
Overnight
Sapa
Meals
Breakfast
Sapa DAY 3

Day 3 — Sapa

Breakfast. Enjoy a guided trek through the spectacular Muong Hoa Valley. Visit: Y Linh Ho Village, Lao Chai Village, Ta Van Village. Meet local ethnic communities while admiring the famous rice terraces. Return to the hotel.

Highlights: Guided trekking tour, entrance fees to villages, private transport.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Immersive
Overnight
Sapa
Meals
Breakfast
Yen Tu DAY 4

Day 4 — Yen Tu

Breakfast. Private transfer to Yen Tu. Upon arrival, check in at Legacy Yen Tu – MGallery, a luxury wellness retreat inspired by traditional Vietnamese architecture. Spend the afternoon enjoying the peaceful atmosphere or relaxing at the spa.

Highlights: Private transfer Sapa to Yen Tu, Legacy Yen Tu check-in, guide.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Relaxed
Overnight
Yen Tu
Meals
Breakfast
Halong Bay DAY 5

Day 5 — Halong Bay

Breakfast. (Optional) Visit Yen Tu Cable Car before continuing to Halong International Cruise Port. Board the Royal Athena Cruise. Activities include: Welcome Drink, Luxury Lunch, Kayaking, Cave Exploration, Sunset Party, Vietnamese Cooking Demonstration. Dinner onboard.

Highlights: Private transfer, Royal Athena Cruise tickets & cabin, onboard activities, kayaking, cave entrance, welcome drink, lunch, dinner.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Immersive
Overnight
Halong Bay
Meals
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Halong Bay DAY 6

Day 6 — Halong Bay

Early Tai Chi session. Breakfast onboard. Continue cruising through Halong Bay. Brunch before disembarkation. Transfer to Novotel Halong Bay. Free afternoon to relax by the beach or swimming pool.

Highlights: Tai Chi session, morning cruise, disembarkation transfer to Novotel Halong Bay.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Relaxed
Overnight
Halong Bay
Meals
Breakfast, Brunch
Da Nang DAY 7

Day 7 — Da Nang

Breakfast. Transfer to Noi Bai Airport. Domestic flight to Danang. Arrival and transfer to InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort. Enjoy the remainder of the day at leisure.

Highlights: Private airport transfer, domestic flight to Danang, resort arrival transfer.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Moderate

Day 8 — Da Nang

Breakfast. Private excursion to Ba Na Hills. Highlights include: Golden Bridge, World’s Longest Cable Car, French Village, Fantasy Park, Le Jardin D’Amour. Return to resort.

Highlights: Private Ba Na Hills excursion, cable car tickets, entrance fees, guide.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Active

Da Nang DAY 9

Day 9 — Da Nang

Breakfast. Transfer to Cam Thanh Coconut Village. Experience: Traditional Basket Boat Ride, Coconut Forest. Continue to Hoi An Ancient Town. Walking tour includes: Japanese Covered Bridge, Ancient Houses, Lantern Streets, Local Market. Free time for shopping and cafés before returning to Danang.

Highlights: Basket boat experience, Cam Thanh coconut forest, Hoi An walking tour tickets, guide, transfers.
Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Immersive
