CHAPTER 01 - THE INVITATION

A Journey Shaped Around Your Group

This journey was designed to leave room for both discovery and rest.

Dear Tina & Friends,

I am delighted to present this privately arranged journey: Tina’s Vietnam Birthday Escape, created for 8 passengers travelling from 27 Mar – 07 Apr 2027. The route unfolds from Hanoi – Halong Bay – Hoi An – Da Nang.

The programme has been considered around a gentler friendly rhythm: early check-in in Hanoi, private guiding and transfers, a luxury overnight cruise, and enough space between active days to pause. Dining, room arrangements and transitions have been planned with care, without adding unnecessary movement.

Please review the journey as a starting point for a personal conversation. Every final detail can be refined around your preferred pace, room choices, and priorities.

CHAPTER 02 · DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY

Day-by-Day Journey Program

Your private 12D11N journey — 12 days, carefully crafted.

Hanoi DAY 1 · 27 Mar 2027

Day 1 - Graceful Hanoi Arrival & Welcome Dinner

Upon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport, you are welcomed by a dedicated concierge representative and escorted by luxury private transfer to Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel. The day remains intentionally light, allowing you and your group time to settle in and refresh. In the evening, a curated welcome dinner introduces Northern Vietnamese cuisine in a refined setting, setting a graceful tone for the journey ahead.

Highlights: Fast-track Arrival Service, Private airport pickup, Welcome Dinner

Notes:
  • Sense of Pace: Relaxed
Overnight

Hanoi DAY 2 · 28 Mar 2027

Day 2 - Historic Hanoi & French Quarter Charm

This half-day introduction to Hanoi focuses on the capital’s essential cultural landmarks at an unhurried pace. With a private historian guide, you visit Ba Dinh Cultural Square, the iconic One Pillar Pagoda, and the tranquil courtyards of the Temple of Literature—Vietnam’s first national university. The afternoon unfolds with a leisurely stroll through the French Quarter's tree-lined avenues, boutique cafes, and the serene shores of Hoan Kiem Lake.

Highlights: Ba Dinh Historic Square, One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature, French Quarter architectural walk.

Notes:
  • Starts at 8am for about 5.5 hours.

Hanoi DAY 3 · 29 Mar 2027

Day 3 - Free Day & Hanoi Old Quarter Food Walk

The day is free at leisure in Hanoi. No service is arranged during this time, including vehicle, guide, activities or meals unless otherwise specified — a chance to rest, wander independently, or simply revisit favorite corners of the city at your own pace.

As the day winds down, set off on a walking food tour through Hanoi's Old Quarter, weaving through lantern-lit streets as the city settles into its nighttime rhythm. Guided by a knowledgeable local host, sample a curated trail of street food and local specialties at cozy corner stalls, wandering past Hoan Kiem Lake and a lively night market, pausing at hidden alleyways known mostly to locals, before strolling back into the Old Quarter, full and content.

Highlights: Old Quarter street food, Night market

Notes:
  • Starts at 05:00pm for about 4 hours.

Halong Bay DAY 4 · 30 Mar 2027

Day 4 - Scenic Ha Long: Luxury Cruise & Limestone Seascape

After breakfast, you travel by private transfer from Hanoi to Ha Long Bay, where the pace begins to slow as the city gives way to coastal scenery. Upon arrival at the marina, board the distinguished Celina of the Sea Cruise and check into your Executive Balcony suite.

Lunch is served onboard as the cruise begins moving through the UNESCO-listed seascape of limestone islands, quiet coves, and open water. The afternoon may include selected cruise activities depending on the confirmed route and weather. As evening falls over Ha Long Bay, you enjoy dinner onboard and spend the night surrounded by one of Vietnam’s most iconic natural landscapes.

Highlights: Ha Long Bay, Luxury cruise, Birthday arrangement.

Notes:
  • Starts at 08:30am

Hoi An DAY 5 · 31 Mar 2027

Day 5 - Seamless Hoi An: Morning Cruise & Bespoke Tailoring

Begin the day with the calm atmosphere of Ha Long Bay, where the cruise may include a light onboard activity, final scenic passage, or relaxed time on deck. After disembarkation, you transfer for a domestic flight to Da Nang, continuing the journey from northern seascapes to Central Vietnam.

Upon arrival, your private chauffeur provides a seamless transfer to Anantara Hoi An Resort. Later, you enjoy a premium bespoke tailoring in Hoi An, a hallmark experience of this historic trading port. The session introduces selected fabrics, silhouettes, and personal styling, creating a refined connection to the town’s artisan tradition.

Highlights: Cruise activities, Premium bespoke tailoring service

Notes:
  • Cruise checks out at 11:00am

Hoi An DAY 6 · 01 Apr 2027

Day 6 - Hoi An Ancient Town Walking Tour & Photography Day

The morning is free at leisure in Hoi An. No service is arranged during this time, including vehicle, guide, activities or meals unless otherwise specified.

In the afternoon, join a guided walking tour through Hoi An Ancient Town, taking in its historic temples, assembly halls, and lantern-lined lanes, including the Museum of Trade Ceramics, Tan Ky Ancient House, Cantonese Assembly Hall, and the iconic Japanese Covered Bridge. A professional photographer accompanies the group throughout, capturing candid portraits amid the town's ancient streets, historic yellow façades, and the scenic Thu Bon River. As evening falls, continue on to the lantern-lit Hoi An Night Market, escorted by your guide and photographer for a few final memorable shots amid the glow of lanterns.

Highlights: Guide & photographer escort · Hoi An Ancient Town · Hoi An Night Market

Notes:
  • Starts at 01:00pm for about 8 hours

Hoi An DAY 7 · 02 Apr 2027

Day 7 - Immersive Hoi An Countryside: Tra Que Cooking & Coconut Basket Boat

Your morning begins in the peaceful Tra Que Vegetable Village, where you join an organic farm-to-table cooking experience surrounded by herb gardens and countryside scenery. Guided by a local chef, you will harvest fresh herbs, learn about traditional ingredients, and prepare authentic Central Vietnamese dishes before enjoying lunch at a farm owner’s home.

In the afternoon, continue to Cam Thanh Coconut Village for a gentle waterway experience through the coconut forest. Board a traditional bamboo basket boat, try paddling with local guidance, and enjoy the lively basket-boat performance that brings a playful touch to the day. Afterward, return comfortably to your hotel in Hoi An.

Highlights: Cooking Class at Tra Que Vegetable Village, Cam Thanh Coconut Village, bamboo basket boat

Notes:
  • Starts at 09:00am for about 7-8 hours
Overnight

Day 8 - Coastal Da Nang: Farewell Moment & Spa Experience

The journey continues to Da Nang, where some friends say goodbye to Vietnam while others continue the coastal chapter of the trip.

Da Nang starts with a relaxing 90-minute Traditional Vietnamese Body Spa experience. After transferring to the beachfront villa, the afternoon is left entirely at leisure. You may relax by the pool, enjoy the sea breeze, explore casually, or simply pause before the next stage begins.

Highlights: Traditional Vietnamese Body Spa, Free afternoon

Notes:
  • Start time depends on departure time

Da Nang DAY 9 · 04 Apr 2027

Day 9 - Elevated Ba Na: Golden Bridge & French Village

Today’s excursion leads to the elevated mountain resort of Ba Na Hills, where cooler air, forested slopes, and panoramic views create a dramatic contrast to the coast. You enjoy priority arrangements for the cable car system before reaching the world-famous Golden Bridge, framed by giant stone hands and mountain scenery.

With your private guide, the visit continues through the French Village, landscaped gardens, viewpoints, and selected leisure areas within the resort complex. The day is paced to allow time for photography and exploration without unnecessary rush. In the late afternoon, you descend by cable car and return to your beachfront villa in Da Nang.

Highlights: Ba Na Hills, Golden Bridge, French Village

Notes:
  • Starts at 08:00am for about 8 hours

Da Nang DAY 10 · 05 Apr 2027

Day 10 – Free Day in Da Nang – City & Resort at Leisure

Today is free at leisure in Da Nang. No service is arranged during this time, including vehicle, guide, activities or meals unless otherwise specified. Take the day to explore the city independently — stroll across the iconic Dragon Bridge, browse the stalls at Han Market, or wander along the golden sands of My Khe Beach at your own pace, discovering the coastal city's laid-back charm.

Alternatively, simply stay in and unwind at the resort, enjoying its pool, spa, and beachfront facilities without a fixed schedule. Whether spent wandering the city or relaxing by the sea, the day is entirely yours to shape as you wish.

Highlights: Free Day

Notes:
  • No service arranged

Da Nang DAY 11 · 06 Apr 2027

Day 11 - Relaxed Da Nang: Ocean Yacht & Barbecue Dinner

Wake up to the soft coastal light of Da Nang. This morning, you will board a private yacht for a two-hour ocean escape, taking in the open sea, sea breeze and quiet rhythm of Vietnam’s central coastline. A small onboard pantry will be prepared for your comfort, with a thoughtful surprise arranged to make the morning feel even more special.

The afternoon is left entirely for relaxation at your villa, giving you time to enjoy the private pool, unwind by the beach or simply slow down together. As evening arrives, a private barbecue dinner will be arranged at the villa, a warm and intimate coastal finale before you say goodbye to Da Nang the following day.

Highlights: Private yacht session, Private barbecue dinner

Notes:
  • Starts at 09:00am for about 3 hours for private yacht session
  • Sense of Pace: Relaxed

Da Nang DAY 12 · 07 Apr 2027

Day 12 - Leisurely Da Nang: Coastal Morning & Onward Departure

Your final morning in Central Vietnam is enjoyed at leisure at Hyatt Regency Danang Resort and Spa, allowing you to savor a beachfront breakfast, stroll along the pristine sands of Non Nuoc Beach, or enjoy a quiet moment by the private pool. At the appropriate time, your private chauffeur provides a seamless transfer to Da Nang International Airport (DAD) for your scheduled departure, concluding a magnificent and unforgettable luxury journey through Vietnam.


Notes:
  • Starts time depends on departure flight time
  • Connecting flight in Hanoi
