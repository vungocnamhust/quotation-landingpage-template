Section 2 — Journey Overview

Prepared for: 02 adults — culturally curious travellers seeking depth,... — A Privately Curated Passage from Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City — Designed...

This privately curated journey has been designed around four defining interests: Vietnam's modern history, imperial heritage, mountain landscapes and refined experiences on the water. Beginning in Hanoi, the route unfolds through the dramatic limestone scenery of Ninh Binh and Lan Ha Bay, before continuing into the terraced valleys and remote communities of Sapa. From there, the journey moves south to the imperial legacy of Hue, the lantern-lit elegance of Hoi An and the dynamic character of Ho Chi Minh City, concluding with the tranquil waterways of the Mekong Delta. The pace is immersive yet unhurried, balancing privately guided discovery with carefully considered time for independent exploration.
