Anisha's Family Trip

A premium, personalized 8-day private journey crafted by Capella Travel for our guests. Day 1 — Sapa

Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport in Hanoi. After a warm welcome and breakfast, board your private vehicle for a scenic drive to the mountainous region of Sapa (approx. 5-6 hours). Arrive at Sapa Jade Hill and check in. Enjoy a relaxed day after a long journey from your hometown.

Highlights: Visa fee included, private airport transfer to Sapa

Day 3 — Sapa

A highlight day in Sapa! Take the Fansipan cable car, the longest three-rope cable car system in the world, to ascend the 'Roof of Indochina' at 3,143 meters. Explore the spiritual pagoda complex at the summit. Enjoy a scenic vegetarian lunch. In the afternoon, visit Ta Van village. Overnight at Sapa Jade Hill.

Highlights: Fansipan cable car roundtrip tickets, guide, Sapa transfers, lunch.

Day 6 — Ninh Binh

Embark on a full-day private excursion to Ninh Binh. Visit the ancient capital of Hoa Lu and climb to the peak of Mua Cave for panoramic views over the rice fields. Return to Hanoi in the late afternoon.

Highlights: Private roundtrip vehicle to Ninh Binh, English guide, Trang An boat tour, Mua Cave entry, vegetarian lunch.
