Day 5 — Sapa

DAY 6

Day 6 — Sapa

A highlight day in Sapa! Take the Fansipan cable car, the longest three-rope cable car system in the world, to ascend the 'Roof of Indochina' at 3,143 meters. Explore the spiritual pagoda complex at the summit. Enjoy a scenic vegetarian lunch. In the afternoon, visit Ta Van village or shop for souvenirs in Sapa town. Spend your last night in Sapa and get ready for the long day tomorrow.

Highlights: Fansipan cable car roundtrip tickets, guide, Sapa transfers, lunch.
