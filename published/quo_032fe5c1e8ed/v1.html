Saudi Family FIT — Luxury Vietnam Family Discovery – 13D12N for Saudi Families

A beautifully paced private Vietnam journey designed especially for a Saudi family seeking comfort, privacy, halal-friendly arrangements, scenic beauty, and relaxing travel days. This 13-day journey showcases Vietnam from North to South, combining Hanoi’s timeless charm, the limestone landscapes of Ninh Binh, the cool mountain scenery of Sapa, a serene overnight cruise in Ha Long Bay, the coastal comfort of Da Nang, the lantern-lit beauty of Hoi An, and the relaxed southern rhythm of Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta. The program is designed for family FIT travel, with private transfers, flexible sightseeing, smooth pacing, child-friendly timing, and halal-conscious meal planning throughout.
