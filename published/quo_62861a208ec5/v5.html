A Privately Arranged Family Journey from Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City

A privately arranged family journey following a graceful north-to-south rhythm through Vietnam. Combining historic Hanoi, the limestone landscapes of Ninh Binh, an overnight cruise in Ha Long Bay, the mountain air of Sapa, the beaches and heritage towns of Central Vietnam, and a meaningful final chapter in Ho Chi Minh City.

CHAPTER 01 - THE INVITATION

A Journey Shaped Around Your Family

This journey was designed to leave room for both discovery and rest.

Dear Family Vacation (2 Pax),

I am delighted to present this privately arranged journey: PROGRAM OVERVIEW, created for Family Vacation (2 Pax) travelling from 01 Apr – 14 Apr 2027. The route unfolds from Hanoi – Ninh Binh – Hanoi – Halong Bay – Hanoi – Sapa – Da Nang – Hoi An – Ho Chi Minh City.

The programme has been considered around a gentler family rhythm: early check-in in Hanoi, private guiding and transfers, a premium overnight train cabin, and enough space between active days to pause. Dining, room arrangements and transitions have been planned with care, without adding unnecessary movement.

Please review the journey as a starting point for a personal conversation. Every final detail can be refined around your preferred pace, room choices and family priorities.
