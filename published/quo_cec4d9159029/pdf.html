Overview

Saudi B2B Partners — GRAND JOURNEY THROUGH THE BEST OF VIETNAM – NORTH TO SOUTH – 13D12N

A beautifully paced journey through Vietnam from North to South, thoughtfully designed for Saudi travelers seeking comfort, scenery, and cultural depth without rushed schedules. This itinerary combines Hanoi’s timeless elegance, the limestone landscapes of Ninh Binh, the cool mountain valleys of Sapa with terraced rice fields and ethnic villages, and a serene overnight cruise in UNESCO-listed Ha Long Bay. The journey continues to Central Vietnam with Da Nang’s coastal comfort and the lantern-lit charm of Hoi An, before concluding in Southern Vietnam with Ho Chi Minh City’s highlights and the peaceful waterways of the Mekong Delta. Private services, balanced sightseeing, generous leisure time, and halal-conscious arrangements make this program ideal for families, couples, honeymooners, and multi-generation travelers.

01

Arrival in Hanoi | Capital City of Vietnam

Upon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport, meet your local team and enjoy a private transfer to your hotel. The remainder of the day is free to rest after the flight or take a gentle stroll through Hanoi’s Old Quarter at your own pace.

02

Hanoi Cultural Discovery | Train Street Coffee Experience

Discover Hanoi’s cultural heart, including Hoan Kiem Lake, elegant French colonial boulevards, and a relaxed walk through the Old Quarter. Enjoy a unique Train Street coffee experience, subject to local regulations, observing daily life unfolding just inches from the tracks.

03

Ninh Binh: “Ha Long Bay on Land” | Scenic Boat Ride

Depart for a full-day excursion to Ninh Binh. Visit Hoa Lu Ancient Capital, enjoy panoramic views from Mua Cave viewpoint, and relax on a scenic Trang An boat ride through limestone valleys and cave tunnels. Return to Hanoi in the late afternoon.
