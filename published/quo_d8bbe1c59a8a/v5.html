CHAPTER 01 - THE INVITATION

A Journey Shaped Around Your Group

This journey was designed to leave room for both discovery and rest.

Dear Tina & Friends,

I am delighted to present this privately arranged journey: Tina’s Vietnam Birthday Escape, created for 11 passengers travelling from 27 Mar – 09 Apr 2027. The route unfolds from Hanoi – Ninh Binh – Halong Bay – Hoi An – Da Nang – Hue – Ho Chi Minh City.

The programme has been considered around a gentler family rhythm: early check-in in Hanoi, private guiding and transfers, a luxury overnight cruise, and enough space between active days to pause. Dining, room arrangements and transitions have been planned with care, without adding unnecessary movement.

Please review the journey as a starting point for a personal conversation. Every final detail can be refined around your preferred pace, room choices, and priorities.
