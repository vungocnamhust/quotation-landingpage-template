Vietnam Safar Demo — Luxury Vietnam Family Discovery – 9D8N for Saudi Families

A premium, family-friendly Vietnam journey designed for Saudi guests, combining Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Ba Na Hills, Hoi An, Hanoi, Ninh Binh, and a Halong Bay cruise option. The program is arranged at a relaxed pace with private transportation, English-speaking guides, halal-friendly dining support, flexible prayer stops, shopping time, and child-suitable cultural experiences. Important routing note: the approved request is 8 nights / 9 days and includes Halong Bay overnight cruise interest. A true overnight Halong Bay cruise normally requires one onboard night, so the operational recommendation is either to add one extra night to make the trip 10D9N or use a same-day luxury Halong Bay cruise within the 9D8N program.
