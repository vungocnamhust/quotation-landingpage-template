Your private 14D13N journey — 14 days, carefully crafted.

Hanoi DAY 1 · 27 Mar 2027

Day 1 - Graceful Hanoi Arrival & Welcome Dinner

Upon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport, you are welcomed by a dedicated concierge representative and escorted by luxury private transfer to Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel. The day remains intentionally light, allowing you and your group time to settle in and refresh. In the evening, a curated welcome dinner introduces Northern Vietnamese cuisine in a refined setting, setting a graceful tone for the journey ahead.

Highlights: Fast-track Arrival Service, Private airport pickup, Welcome Dinner

Hanoi DAY 2 · 28 Mar 2027

Day 2 - Heritage Hanoi: Mausoleum, Pagoda & Temple

This half-day introduction to Hanoi focuses on the capital’s essential cultural landmarks at an unhurried pace. With a private historian guide, you visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, One Pillar Pagoda, and the elegant Temple of Literature, Vietnam’s first national university. The experience offers a thoughtful perspective on national memory, Confucian heritage, and the ceremonial rhythm of old Hanoi.

Highlights: Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature
Notes:
  • Starts at 8am for about 5.5 hours.

Hanoi DAY 3 · 29 Mar 2027

Day 3 - Local Hanoi by Jeep: Cuisine, Backstreets & Train Street

Your Hanoi Jeep Tour begins with hotel pick-up in the Hanoi Old Quarter, followed by a safety briefing with your guide and driver. The journey introduces both the elegant and hidden sides of the capital, passing the Hanoi Opera House, local backstreets, alleyways, markets, homes and everyday neighbourhoods that reveal how Hanoians truly live, work and gather.

The route continues past major landmarks such as the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Thanh Nien Road, West Lake, Truc Bach Lake, Banana Island, and Tran Quoc Pagoda. You also explore quieter local areas, bonsai streets, hidden lanes and the B52 aircraft site, before ending with authentic Hanoi dishes at selected local restaurants and food stalls.

Highlights: Local food, St. Joseph’s Cathedral, French Quarter, Hanoi Opera House, Long Bien Bridge, West Lake, Train Street
Notes:
  • Starts at 08:00am for about 5 hours.

Halong Bay DAY 4 · 30 Mar 2027

Day 4 - Scenic Ha Long: Luxury Cruise & Limestone Seascape

After breakfast, you travel by private transfer from Hanoi to Ha Long Bay, where the pace begins to slow as the city gives way to coastal scenery. Upon arrival at the marina, board the distinguished Celina of the Sea Cruise and check into your Executive Balcony suite.

Lunch is served onboard as the cruise begins moving through the UNESCO-listed seascape of limestone islands, quiet coves, and open water. The afternoon may include selected cruise activities depending on the confirmed route and weather. As evening falls over Ha Long Bay, you enjoy dinner onboard and spend the night surrounded by one of Vietnam’s most iconic natural landscapes.

Highlights: Ha Long Bay, Luxury cruise, Birthday arrangement.

Hoi An DAY 6 · 01 Apr 2027

Day 6 - Immersive Hoi An: Ancient Town, Lanterns & Riverfront

Your afternoon begins with pick-up from your Hoi An hotel before heading to Cam Thanh Coconut Village. Here, you enjoy a peaceful boat trip through the coconut forest, try paddling a traditional bamboo basket boat, and watch a lively basket-boat performance before continuing to Hoi An Ancient Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

In Hoi An, you explore key cultural landmarks including the Traditional Performing Arts House, Museum of Trade Ceramics, Tan Ky Ancient House, Cantonese Assembly Hall, and the iconic Japanese Covered Bridge. As evening falls, enjoy the lantern-lit Hoi An Night Market, a local specialty dinner, then take a gentle Hoai River boat ride and release flower lanterns for good luck.

Highlights: Cam Thank Coconut Village, Hoi An Ancient Town, Hoi An Night Market, Hoai River
Notes:
  • Starts at 02:00pm for about 6 hours

Hoi An DAY 7 · 02 Apr 2027

Day 7 - Relaxed Hoi An: Memories & Traditional Dance

The day begins with a specialized photography experience in Hoi An Ancient Town, arranged during the best available light for portraits of you and your friends. The session captures your group against lantern-lined streets, heritage façades, quiet alleys, and the riverside scenery of the Thu Bon River, creating a personal visual memory of the journey.

On the afternoon, the day speeds up by a private traditional dance session. The Premium Vietnamese Folk Dance Experience invites your group into the grace and colour of Vietnamese tradition through movement, music, costume and storytelling. Guided by professional instructors and a bilingual host, participants learn simple folk dance gestures using conical hats, fans, scarves and lanterns, then come together for a joyful final performance.

Highlights: Hoi An Ancient Town, Photography experience, Folk Dance Experience

  • Starts at 09:00am for about 8 hours

Da Nang DAY 9 · 04 Apr 2027

Day 9 - Elevated Ba Na: Golden Bridge & French Village

Today’s excursion leads to the elevated mountain resort of Ba Na Hills, where cooler air, forested slopes, and panoramic views create a dramatic contrast to the coast. You enjoy priority arrangements for the cable car system before reaching the world-famous Golden Bridge, framed by giant stone hands and mountain scenery.

With your private guide, the visit continues through the French Village, landscaped gardens, viewpoints, and selected leisure areas within the resort complex. The day is paced to allow time for photography and exploration without unnecessary rush. In the late afternoon, you descend by cable car and return to your beachfront villa in Da Nang.

Highlights: Ba Na Hills, Golden Bridge, French Village

Hue DAY 10 · 05 Apr 2027

Day 10 – Imperial Hue: Citadel, Royal Tombs & River Heritage

Depart from Da Nang City in the morning and journey north toward Hue, following one of Central Vietnam’s most scenic coastal routes. Cross the legendary Hai Van Pass, where mountain roads open to sweeping views of the sea, then pause at Lap An Lagoon and Lang Co Beach, known for calm water, mountain reflections and a long white-sand shoreline. Enjoy lunch near Lang Co before continuing into Hue’s imperial landscape.

In the afternoon, explore the Hue Imperial City, the former royal seat of the Nguyen Dynasty, including the historic Imperial Citadel. Continue to Khai Dinh King’s Tomb, admired for its blend of Eastern artistry and Western architecture. Time permitting, visit Thien Mu Pagoda before returning comfortably to Da Nang.

Highlights: Hai Van Pass, Lang Co Beach, Imperial Citadel, Khai Dinh King’s Tomb
Notes:
  • Starts at 08:00am for about 9 hours (Dress code: Long pants)

Day 13 - Immersive Mekong: Private River Journey & Local Crafts

The day is devoted to an immersive journey into the Mekong Delta, one of Vietnam’s most important agricultural and riverine regions. Travelling by private river vessel, you move through calm waterways, coconut groves, and small canals, observing daily life closely connected to the water.

After lunch in a refined local setting, the programme continues with selected visits to artisanal workshops, family-run producers, fruit gardens, or traditional craft stops. The experience is curated for comfort and authenticity rather than mass tourism, offering a polished yet meaningful view of southern Vietnam before returning to Ho Chi Minh City.

Highlights: Ben Tre, Mekong Delta, Boat trip
