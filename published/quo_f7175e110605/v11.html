Dear Tina & Friends,

I am delighted to present this privately arranged journey: Tina’s Vietnam Birthday Escape, created for 11 passengers  travelling from 27 Mar – 09 Apr 2027. The route unfolds from Hanoi – Halong Bay – Hoi An – Da Nang – Hue – Ho Chi Minh City.

Your private 14D13N journey — 14 days, carefully crafted.

Hanoi DAY 1

Day 1 - Graceful Hanoi Arrival & Welcome Dinner

Upon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport, you are welcomed by a dedicated concierge representative and escorted by luxury private transfer to Peridot Grand Luxury Boutique Hotel. The day remains intentionally light, allowing you and your group time to settle in and refresh. In the evening, a curated welcome dinner introduces Northern Vietnamese cuisine in a refined setting, setting a graceful tone for the journey ahead.

Highlights: Private airport pickup · Welcome Dinner.

Hanoi DAY 2

Day 2 — Hanoi

This half-day introduction to Hanoi focuses on the capital’s essential cultural landmarks at an unhurried pace. With a private historian guide, you visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, One Pillar Pagoda, and the elegant Temple of Literature, Vietnam’s first national university. The experience offers a thoughtful perspective on national memory, Confucian heritage, and the ceremonial rhythm of old Hanoi.

Highlights: Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, One Pillar Pagoda, Temple of Literature
