Hanoi DAY 2 · 31 Mar 2027

Day 2: Scenic Ninh Binh: Ancient Capitals & Karst Waterways

Depart Hanoi behind as you journey south through picturesque rural landscapes toward the historical region of Ninh Binh. Begin your morning exploration at Hoa Lu Ancient Capital, where you will wander through historic sanctuary temples built in honor of the ancient Dinh and Le royal dynasties while learning about Vietnam’s rich royal legacy amidst majestic rising limestone cliffs.

After enjoying a delicious local lunch featuring traditional regional specialties, proceed to the breathtaking UNESCO-listed Trang An Scenic Landscape Complex. Board a hand-rowed sampan boat to glide along emerald waterways, drifting beneath towering karst peaks, navigating hidden river caves, and taking in serene countryside views before relaxing on your comfortable private transfer back to Hanoi.

Hanoi DAY 5 · 03 Apr 2027

Day 5: Historic Hanoi Heritage & Overnight Express to Sapa

Begin your immersive city exploration visiting the solemn Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex, learning about the nation's historic founding leader before admiring the unique lotus-inspired architecture of One Pillar Pagoda. Continue your cultural journey to the ancient Temple of Literature, Vietnam's very first national university established in 1070, featuring historic courtyards, stone steles, and beautiful traditional pavilions.

Following a tasty local lunch, visit the serene Tran Quoc Pagoda situated peacefully on the shores of West Lake. Walk around romantic Hoan Kiem Lake, browse bustling stalls at Dong Xuan Market, and experience the iconic Hanoi Train Street. In the evening, transfer to the railway station to board the comfortable Chapa Express Train for your overnight journey to Sapa.

Sapa DAY 7 · 05 Apr 2027

Day 7: Active Sapa Highlands: Ethnic Culture & Sky-High Passes

Embark on a captivating cultural trek down into the scenic valley to visit traditional Cat Cat Village, home to the Black Hmong ethnic community. Discover authentic timber stilt houses, observe age-old local handicrafts like indigo dyeing, and admire the cascading rushing waters of Tien Sa Waterfall set against dramatic terraced rice paddies before sitting down for lunch.

In the afternoon, travel along scenic mountain roads up to the breathtaking O Quy Ho Mountain Pass, famous for its sweeping high-altitude panoramic views. Next, test your courage at the thrilling Rong May Glass Bridge suspended high above the valley, and admire the thunderous cascading waters of majestic Silver Waterfall before returning comfortable back to Sapa town.

Highlights: Cat Cat Village, Tien Sa Waterfall, O Quy Ho Mountain Pass, Rong May Glass Bridge, Silver Waterfall

Da Nang DAY 9 · 07 Apr 2027

Day 9: Coastal Da Nang Exploration: Marble Peaks & Son Tra Sanctuary

Start your morning exploring the mystical limestone peaks of Marble Mountains, climbing stone stairways to discover hidden cave grottoes and historic Buddhist sanctuaries. Afterwards, cross the iconic Dragon Bridge spanning the river and visit the renowned Museum of Cham Sculpture to admire its magnificent collection of ancient regional stone carving artifacts before enjoying a delicious coastal lunch.

In the afternoon, journey along the picturesque coastal winding roads of the Son Tra Peninsula, enjoying sweeping views of the sparkling ocean. Visit the revered Linh Ung Pagoda, home to the towering white Lady Buddha statue overlooking the bay, while taking in peaceful panoramic views across the coastline before returning comfortable to your hotel for a relaxing evening.

Highlights: Marble Mountains, Dragon Bridge, Museum of Cham Sculpture, Son Tra Peninsula, Linh Ung Pagoda

Hoi An DAY 10 · 08 Apr 2027

Day 10: Charming Hoi An Magic: Coconut Palms & Lantern-Lit Waterways

Depart Da Nang at midday for the lush eco-sanctuary of Cam Thanh Coconut Village, where you will board a traditional bamboo basket boat to navigate peaceful palm-fringed waterways. Learn local fishing techniques and enjoy a fun-filled excursion through green waterways before making your way to the enchanting UNESCO town of Hoi An for a delicious regional lunch.

Explore UNESCO-listed Hoi An Ancient Town, admiring the historic Tan Ky Ancient House, the colorful Cantonese Assembly Hall, and the iconic Japanese Covered Bridge. In the evening, wander the vibrant Hoi An Night Market, savor local dishes for dinner, and embark on a scenic boat ride along the Hoai River to release glowing flower lanterns onto the water.

Ho Chi Minh City DAY 12 · 10 Apr 2027

Day 12: Transition to Saigon & Cu Chi Historical Tunnels

Transfer to Da Nang International Airport for your short domestic flight south to bustling Ho Chi Minh City. Upon arrival at Tan Son Nhat International Airport, meet your driver and guide for a comfortable transfer into the dynamic city center, where you will savor a delicious Southern Vietnamese lunch before departing for your afternoon historical countryside excursion.

Travel northwest to explore the fascinating underground network of the historic Cu Chi Tunnels. Discover how local soldiers lived and fought in these remarkable subterranean passages, view hidden trapdoors, medical bunkers, and supply rooms, and gain deep insight into Vietnam's wartime history before returning back to the vibrant center of Ho Chi Minh City in late afternoon.

Ho Chi Minh City DAY 13 · 11 Apr 2027

Day 13: Immersive Mekong Delta: Waterways & Orchards of Ben Tre

Depart the city early heading south into the fertile tropical heart of the Mekong Delta. Arrive in the picturesque province of Ben Tre Waterways, famous for its coconut groves and tranquil canals. Board a local river boat to cruise along quiet tributaries, visiting artisanal coconut processing workshops and tasting fresh tropical fruits while immersing in rural life.

Enjoy a traditional Delta lunch at a quiet garden restaurant featuring regional fish specialties. Afterwards, take a peaceful hand-rowed sampan ride through shade-dappled narrow canals fringed with water coconut palms. Explore local village pathways by bicycle or motorized rickshaw before relaxing on your comfortable private transfer back to Ho Chi Minh City in the late afternoon.

Day 14: Heritage Saigon Highlights & Evening Departure

Begin your final morning city tour admiring the elegant French colonial architecture of Saigon Notre-Dame Basilica and the historic Central Post Office, designed by Gustave Eiffel. Continue your cultural journey with an insightful visit to the War Remnants Museum, exploring poignant historical exhibits and photography collections that offer a profound perspective on Vietnam's modern history.

After finishing your morning city tour and enjoying lunch, return to your hotel to rest, pack, and prepare for your homeward journey. In the late afternoon, your private driver will pick you up for a comfortable transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport for your departure flight home, concluding your unforgettable grand adventure through Vietnam.
