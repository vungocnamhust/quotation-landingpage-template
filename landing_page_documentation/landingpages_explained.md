### Explanations
A Wall of Love is a page that curates your best customer testimonial and reviews. You can then link to the Wall of Love on your website, your social channels, and in your marketing and sales emails. By showcasing testimonials you build trust and credibility and turn visitors into customers.

Walls of Love have become more popular in recent years as businesses are having to working harder to build trust and stand out in a crowded market and high ad costs.

In this teardown I highlight some of the key ideas for a Wall of Love. The main thing is to use structure and highlights to make it the Wall more scan-able, as otherwise it will be overwhelming.

And add call-to-actions everywhere to drive action from the page, otherwise the page is pretty pointless.

The idea is to build a connection to the visitor, so talking to them as human not a brand is important.However, I've never tested this.Either way there's lots to love about this page - great work Jay.
